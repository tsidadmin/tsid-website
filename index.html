Grow · In depth

Consultancy, with agents built in.

Every Grow service is ordinary consultancy made faster and more consistent by AI, automation and agents working underneath it. Here's what each one actually involves.

G01

Sales Outbound & Appointment Setting

Your pipeline shouldn't depend on whoever remembered to follow up. We build and run multi-channel outbound — email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp — where agents research each prospect, personalise every message and keep the cadence going until a meeting lands in your calendar.

  • Ideal-customer profiling, lead scraping & data enrichment
  • AI-personalised sequences across email, LinkedIn & WhatsApp
  • Automated follow-ups and reply handling
  • Qualified appointments booked straight into your calendar
  • Weekly reporting on opens, replies and meetings set

✦ Agents handle the research, personalisation and chasing — your team steps in when a real conversation starts.

G02

Marketing & Branding

Positioning first, then content that sounds like you — consistently. We define the brand, then wire automation behind it: content pipelines, scheduling and AI-assisted drafting with a human editor, so the message shows up every week without needing a big team behind it.

  • Positioning, messaging & brand identity
  • Content engine — AI-drafted, human-edited, always on-brand
  • Campaign setup across the channels that matter to you
  • Marketing automation for the repetitive, weekly work
  • Performance dashboards you can actually read

✦ Automation runs research, drafts, scheduling and reporting — the thinking stays human.

G03

Recruitment & Talent

Hiring slows down when screening eats the week. Our agent-assisted pipeline sources candidates across channels, parses CVs into structured profiles automatically, screens against your criteria and keeps candidates warm — so your shortlist arrives ready to interview.

  • Role scoping and sourcing strategy
  • Multi-channel candidate sourcing
  • Automated CV parsing & screening — see the demo above
  • Interview scheduling and pipeline tracking
  • Offer and onboarding support

✦ The same CV-intake agent in our case studies runs inside this service.

G04

Immigration

Work passes and relocation, handled with the paperwork discipline they demand. We advise founders, teams and talent moving into Singapore and around the region — eligibility, documentation, timelines — with automation tracking every deadline and document so nothing slips.

  • Work-pass eligibility assessment and strategy
  • Document preparation with automated checklists
  • Application tracking, renewals & expiry alerts
  • Relocation and dependant-pass advisory
  • Ongoing compliance calendar for your team

✦ Deadlines, renewals and document checklists are monitored automatically — humans handle the judgement calls.

G05

Finance & Advisory

Decisions are only as good as the numbers behind them. We combine financial advisory with automated reporting — clean books, live dashboards and forecasts that update themselves — so you see the business as it is, not as it was last quarter.

  • Financial health review and planning
  • Cash-flow forecasting and scenario modelling
  • Automated reporting & real-time dashboards
  • Budgeting support with live variance tracking
  • Funding-readiness and growth planning

✦ The accounting agent in our case studies keeps the numbers current underneath the advice.

G06

Business Matching

Growth often comes down to who you're connected to. We match you with vetted partners, suppliers and channels in Singapore and the region — using enriched, scored data rather than someone's rolodex — then help you build the systems to manage those relationships as they scale.

  • Partner, supplier and channel identification
  • AI-scored matching from scraped & enriched datasets
  • Vetting and warm introductions
  • Market-entry guidance for the region
  • CRM and systems to manage relationships after the match

✦ Data does the longlisting; people make the introductions.

Education Consultancy

  • Custom curriculum built around your real workflows
